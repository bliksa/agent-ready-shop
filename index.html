Can AI agents buy from your store?

A/SHOP — short for Agent Ready Shop — is a free audit for online stores. Shopping agents such as ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity don’t click through a site — they read it through files, feeds and APIs. The audit shows what an agent can and can’t do with your store and scores it from 0 to 100 (the AIO score).

What the audit checks

  • AI crawler policy — whether robots.txt actually allows shopping and answer agents (OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Claude-User, PerplexityBot).
  • Product structured data — Schema.org Product / Offer JSON-LD with price, availability and GTIN.
  • llms.txt manifest — a Markdown map of the store at /llms.txt for language models.
  • Machine-readable product feed — a feed or sitemap agents can enumerate.
  • Structured product API — an API and discovery document for live price, stock and details.
  • Agent-commerce layer — a validated commerce manifest, Agentic Commerce Protocol (ACP) or MCP discovery.

The WordPress plugin

The free Agent Ready Shop plugin for WordPress (WooCommerce and other store plugins) writes and maintains llms.txt, outputs clean Product schema, publishes a product feed and a read-only commerce API with a discovery manifest, and sets an explicit AI-crawler policy.
